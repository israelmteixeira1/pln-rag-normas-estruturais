## Scope

This  Standard  provides  minimum  loads  for  the  design  of  structures,  regardless  their  class  and destination,  except  for  the  cases  covered  by  specific  Brazilian  standards  (ABNT  NBR  6123, ABNT NBR 15421, ABNT NBR 14323 e ABNT NBR 15200).
